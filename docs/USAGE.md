# How to use Duo Wallet

Short companion to the [README](../README.md).

## First launch

1. Enter your Supabase project URL and anon key (or use a build that already has them).
2. Sign up with your name, email and password.
3. Add at least one **account** (M-Pesa, bank, cash) and its **opening balance**.

## Share with your partner

1. Settings → **Household** → create a household.
2. Copy or share the **invite code**.
3. Partner installs the APK, signs up, joins with the code.
4. Switch between **Personal** and the household from the ledger button on Home.

On a shared ledger, Home → **Together** shows:

- combined balance of both of you
- each person's opening balance and current balance

## Log money

Tap **+**:

- **Spent** — expense (add the **transaction fee** if M-Pesa/bank charged you; fees are spending)
- **Received** — income
- **Moved** — between your accounts
- **When** — date and time (kept for accountability)
- On a household ledger, pick **Who** spent it

## Salary, debts, budgets

| Screen | Path |
| --- | --- |
| Salary & fixed bills | Settings → Salary & bills |
| Debts | Settings → Debts |
| Budgets | Settings → Budgets |
| Theme (light / dark) | Settings → Theme |

Salary posts automatically on its day of the month. To repay a debt, log a spend and choose **Pays a debt**.

## Tips

- Pull down to refresh any list.
- Activity is the full month; Home shows a recent snapshot.
- Theme can follow the phone or stay locked to light/dark.
